# Just Epic Portals

Linked portals for Minecraft **1.21.1** on NeoForge **21.1.243 or newer**. Install the mod on the server and every client.

- Build a frame from one of twenty-eight vanilla blocks, with one matching Portal Anchor anywhere along its edge. Openings run from 1 x 2 up to 9 x 9, upright in any direction, flat in a floor or overhead. Corners are optional.
- Link two anchors with a Portal Anchor Core (PAC). The two ends may use different materials, sizes and orientations, in the same dimension or across dimensions.
- Travel is instant and brings mounts, occupied vehicles and leashed animals. A per-anchor Nearby option also brings mobs within four blocks.
- Each anchor's owner chooses who may travel: Self, Team or Everyone. Teams come from vanilla scoreboard teams, FTB Teams or Open Parties and Claims.
- Operators and pack makers can require advancements (vanilla, modded or datapack ones), per anchor or for every portal of a material.
- Dyes recolor a portal surface, and activating items switch between eleven animated portal effects. JEI shows the recipes and designs.

This repository is the mod's source, published for reference. Releases are on CurseForge.
